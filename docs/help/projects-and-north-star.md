# Projects and north star

Keeptrack stays lightweight: nothing here is required, and older boards keep working.

## Client north star

A **north star** is a short note about what you are trying to solve for a client and what success looks like.

1. On the board, double-click a **client** name on a card (or open a person and use their company).
2. Edit **North star** and the company file links in the client panel.

The same text is stored in `client_info` for that company name.

## Projects under a client

A client can have several **projects**. Each project has a name, its own north star, a status (active, done, or paused), document links, and people (by email).

1. Open the client panel and choose **Add project**, or add one from a task.
2. Open a project to see open tasks, links, and people.
3. When adding people, the board suggests colleagues whose person record lists that client as their company. Click to add; nothing is added automatically.

## Tasks and projects

On a task, set **Project** in the edit drawer (optional). Use the **Project** filter on the board to focus on one project.

## Board members as people

If you use CRM mode, add yourself as a person so follow-ups and company links stay consistent. After you save a GitHub token, the board may offer **Add yourself as a person**. If you skip it, a small reminder appears until you dismiss it or add a person with your GitHub login or email.
