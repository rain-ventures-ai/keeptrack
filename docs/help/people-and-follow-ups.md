# People and follow-ups

## Today
**Today** is the first page. It combines people to contact with overdue and due tasks, according to the board sections you have enabled. People appear in these groups:
- **Overdue:** the next-step date is in the past.
- **Today:** the next-step date is today.
- **Next 7 days.**
- **No next step:** an open person with no date. Give each one a next step, so that nobody is forgotten.
- **Gone quiet:** no contact for 30 days.
- **Tasks due** (if you use tasks).

People at the stages **Won** and **Lost** are not shown on Today.

Click a person to open their card.

## Prep for call
On a board with more than one member, Today has a **Prep for call** tile. Select it, then select the person you have a call with. The board makes a short brief from its own data. It uses no AI and sends nothing anywhere. The brief has these groups, and each card shows in one group only:

- **Goals:** open cards with the label `goal` or a title that starts with "Goal".
- **To discuss:** open cards such as "Discuss pricing with Sam": a word like discuss, agree, decide, ask, check or review, then "with" and a name. The name must be one of you, and the card must belong to the other person or to nobody. A short name matches when its first two letters match the member's name or login (Ollie matches Oliver).
- **Waiting on one of you**, **Overdue or stuck**, **Asked of each other this week** (comments from the last 7 days where one of you mentions the other), **Shared cards** (assigned to both of you, not in Backlog) and **Done in the last 7 days**.
- The number of items in the **Review Inbox**.

**Copy brief** copies the brief as text. **Copy for AI** adds a prompt that asks Claude, Codex or ChatGPT to make a short agenda from it. The board remembers the last person you selected, in this browser only.

## Add a person
Click **Add…** to open the form. **Name** is required; company, role, email, phone and LinkedIn are optional. After the person is added, their card opens so you can add a stage, next step, notes or anything else.

For a one-line shortcut, type **Name | Company | Role | Email** in the add box and press Enter.

## A person's card
- **Name** and **stage** at the top.
- **Next step** and its **date**. Use **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **+1 week** to set the date quickly, or **×** on the date for no date. Always give a date: Today uses it.
- **Client, role, value, source and GitHub** in the same list.
- **Emails** and **Phones:** as many as you need, each with a label. Click **+ Add email** or **+ Add phone**. The mail and phone icons write an email or call.
- **Contact log:** each message, email, call and meeting.
- **Notes**, **Tasks**, **Links** and the company's **Files**.
- **History:** who changed what, and when.

Each field saves on its own when you leave it. There is no Save button.

## Log a contact
1. In **Contact log**, write what you sent or talked about.
2. Select the channel: LinkedIn, Email, Call, Meeting or Note.
3. Click **Log it**.

When you log a contact for a person at the first stage (**New**), the person moves to **Contacted**.

## Drafts
A draft is a message that you have not sent yet. It does **not** count as contact.
1. Paste the draft into the contact log and select **Draft (not sent yet)**. AI assistants log their drafts in the same way.
2. Click **📋 Copy** on the draft and paste it into LinkedIn or your email.
3. Send it yourself.
4. Click **Mark sent**. Type the date for the follow-up, or leave it empty.

## Copy for AI
**🤖 Copy for AI** at the top of a card (also in its **⋯** menu) copies the person, their stage, their next step and their recent contact log. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant and ask for a message. See [AI assistants](ai-assistants.md).

## Search
**People** lists everyone. Search by name, company, notes or next step, or select a stage.

## Delete a person
Open the **⋯** (More actions) menu at the top of the card, select **Delete person** and confirm the warning. Use the **Undo** button, **Ctrl+Z** on Windows/Linux, or **⌘Z** on a Mac if you change your mind during the same board session. See [Privacy and security](privacy-and-security.md) for what stays in the repo history.
