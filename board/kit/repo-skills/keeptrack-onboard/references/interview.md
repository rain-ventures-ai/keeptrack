# The interview

Ask one question at a time. Accept short answers. "Skip", "later", "don't know" and "just do it" are good answers: use the default and go on. Never ask a question that an earlier answer already answered.

| # | Question | Why | Default if skipped |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Name your clients.** (Companies or people you work with now. Five names is enough.) | These are the records the person cares about. A first board can be only this. | Find candidates in the places in question 2, and ask the person to tick them. |
| 2 | **Where are their files and emails?** (For example: "Drive, folder Clients" or "my Mac, Documents/Work", "Gmail".) | The places to read. Files stay there. | Search the connected file store for folders with the client names. |
| 3 | Who is the main contact at each client? | People records with a name, not only a company. | Find the person you email most at that domain (only if email is connected). |
| 4 | Any leads or old clients you want on the board too? | Fills the pipeline. | None now. They can be added later. |
| 5 | Where are your to-dos today? (Trello, Google Tasks, Todoist, a notebook, nowhere.) | Decides if tasks are imported. | Do not import tasks. |
| 6 | Do you want to stop using that tool? | If no, link it, do not import it. | No: link only. |
| 7 | Who else works with you? (GitHub usernames if they will use the board.) | Assignees. | Only the owner. |
| 8 | What makes someone a client for you? (Signed, paid, a meeting?) | Tunes the scoring in `finding-clients.md`. | Paid, or 3 or more meetings, or 10 or more emails sent in 12 months. |

After question 2 you can stop and go to the survey. Offer the rest as: "I have enough to start. Do you want to answer 6 more short questions, or go now?"

Save the answers in `onboarding/interview.md`, one line per question, with "skipped" where the person skipped. You can ask a skipped question again later, at the time it matters (for example question 6 when you find a Trello board).

## Good and bad
- Good: "Name your clients." Person: "Acme, Northwind, Hurst College." Agent: "Thanks. Where are their files?"
- Bad: a form of 15 questions at the start. Busy people stop.
- Bad: asking for an id, a URL format or a stage name. Find it yourself.
