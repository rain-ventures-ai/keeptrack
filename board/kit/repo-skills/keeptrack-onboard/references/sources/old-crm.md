# An old CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Capsule, Zoho, Salesforce)

People, companies, deals and stages. Value: high if the person is leaving it.

A person who uses their CRM well will probably stay on it. The useful case: they pay for it and do not use it.

**Read it:** the CRM's CSV export of contacts, companies and deals (HubSpot: Settings > Import & Export > Export). A connector only if one is connected.

**Map it:** deal stage to Keeptrack stage (show the mapping and ask once); deal amount to `value`; owner to the assignee only if that person will use the board. Bring open deals and current clients; leave closed-lost deals older than 12 months.

**Traps:** thousands of contacts from a form or an import: bring only those with a deal, an owner, or an activity in the last 12 months.
