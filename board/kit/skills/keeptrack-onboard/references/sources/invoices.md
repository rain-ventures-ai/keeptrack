# Invoices and accounts (Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, Sage)

Who pays, so who is a client. The strongest sign. Value: high.

**Read it:** ask the person to export the contacts list or the sales invoices as CSV (in Xero: Contacts > Export; in QuickBooks: Sales > Customers > Export; in FreeAgent: Contacts > Export). Use a connector only if one exists and is connected.

**Use:** customer name, email, last invoice date, and whether any invoice was paid in the period. Do not copy amounts into the board unless the person asks (then `value` on the person, as a plain number).

**Traps:** suppliers in the same contacts list (keep customers only); one customer with several billing names.
