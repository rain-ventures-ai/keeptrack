# Calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook)

Meetings with outside people. A meeting is strong proof of a relation. Value: high.

**Read it:** the Google Calendar connector (list events in the period). Outlook: a Microsoft connector, or skip.

**Use:** the attendees outside the owner's own domain, the date, and the event title only when it names the client. Count meetings per domain for `../finding-clients.md`. The next future meeting can be a person's next step (`next`: "Meeting: <title>", `due`: its date), but ask first.

**Traps:** internal events, all-hands, webinars with many attendees (ignore events with more than 10 outside attendees), and a personal or family calendar (do not open without a yes).
